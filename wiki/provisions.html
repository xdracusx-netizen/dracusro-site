The war

Caravans Sieges

Payon sells the food and potions the rest of the server runs on. You do not cook — you buy.

There is no cooking skill and nothing to level. The NPCs do it. You walk up, pay, and walk away with the item.

Two buff slots

SlotWhat goes in it
FoodOne stat dish, or one all-stat dish. Not both.
PotionOne buff potion.
  • Using a second replaces the first within a slot, immediately.
  • Food and potions are independent. Both apply.
  • Both persist through death. They are expensive.
  • Both are lost on class change.

Standard Ragnarok lets food and potions stack without limit, which turns preparation into a checklist longer than the fight. Two slots makes it a decision.

Stat food

The existing level 1 to 10 dishes, then the higher tier at Metropolis. All flat stats, twenty minutes, unchanged from the base game.

Concentration, Awakening and Berserk potions are not sold here. They are ordinary vendor stock, available in every town. Payon sells what a shopkeeper cannot.

Siegebreaker is the answer to turtling

Penetration ignores a share of the target's damage reduction, which makes it devastating against somebody who stacked defensively and close to worthless against somebody who did not.

AgainstExtra damage from 20% penetration
A heavily stacked paladinAbout +65%
A typical geared playerAbout +50%
Somebody in light gearAbout +8%

A flat damage potion helps equally against everybody, which means it raises the floor rather than countering anything. Siegebreaker punishes the specific behaviour and nothing else — and if you brought it against a squishy target, you wasted the slot.

Why no reduction potions

Damage modifiers are additive and get diluted into a pool that is already enormous. Reduction is multiplicative and compounds against a wall. A 20% damage potion adds about 6% real damage; a 12% reduction potion is the full 12%. There are no reduction consumables on this server and there never will be.

The surcharge becomes civic contribution for Payon. Nobody's own city loses anything — Payon simply grows on everybody else's consumable habit.

Town and City food is not for farming. It is priced so that a guild buys it before a siege or a dungeon push, not to grind field maps. Tiers 1 and 2 are cheap enough to run continuously.

Provisioning

Payon's faction research makes their own citizens' consumables stronger and longer-lasting, up to 30% at full rank. It applies wherever they are and does not transfer with the item — buy a dish from a Payon player and you get the standard effect.
