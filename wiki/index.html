How it fits together

Nothing here is a side system. Every piece feeds the next one, and that is the whole design.

  • You kill things. Experience goes to you, civic contribution goes to your city, and the kill counts toward your bestiary.
  • Your city grows on what its citizens actually played. Each tier opens deeper dungeons, better food, and eventually research.
  • Your city commissions caravans with that contribution. Escorting and raiding them is where zeny comes from, and where your faction's military points come from.
  • Military points fund wars. Declaring, siege equipment, and every respawn on a Saturday afternoon.
  • Dungeons and world bosses drop materials that crafting turns into gear — and you need every faction's dungeon to make anything.
  • Winning territory opens tier 5, and tier 5 is the only thing on the server that cannot be bought.

Which means there is no wasted activity. Somebody grinding a field map alone is funding the convoy that arms the guild that takes the castle.
