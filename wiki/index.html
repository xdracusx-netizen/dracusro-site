How it fits together

Nothing here is a side system. Every piece feeds the next one, and that is the whole design.

  • You kill things. Experience goes to you, contribution goes to your city, and the kill counts toward your bestiary.
  • Your city grows on what its citizens actually played. Each tier opens deeper dungeons, a world boss, and better crafting.
  • Dungeons and bosses drop materials that the crafting your city unlocked turns into the best gear available.
  • Guilds run caravans between cities. That trade is where war supply comes from, and anyone can try to take one off the road.
  • Supply funds sieges. Castles change hands, territory generates income, and the income feeds back into the cities.

Which means there is no wasted activity. Somebody grinding a field map alone is developing the city that opens the dungeon that arms the guild that takes the castle.
