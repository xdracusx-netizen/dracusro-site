The war

Caravans Sieges
You have this from your first login. No quest, no unlock, no NPC. Your character simply wakes up with it.

Two collection systems that make every kill matter. There are no quest chains on this server, so something else has to make one map worth choosing over another.

What it belongs to

RuleDetail
Per characterNot account-wide. Progress belongs to the character that earned it.
Across all classesKilling as a Wizard progresses the same codex you use as a Knight.
No decayNothing expires or resets.
No transferProgress cannot be moved between characters.

Which is the point. With multiclassing, your main is your alt — there is no second character to spread progress across.

What the milestones give

MilestoneReward
M1 to M5Base experience and job experience, scaled to the monster
M6A permanent stat bonus, themed to that monster
M7A permanent HP bonus — 33 for normals, up to 99 for MVPs

Job experience is why this matters across your whole character. Base level is shared between all twelve classes; job level is not. Without job experience the codex would help your first class and nothing after it.

Milestones are claimed once per character, so reaching M5 on Poring as a Knight does not award again as a Wizard. With 1,142 species there is always something left to claim.

The Card Album

1,023 obtainable cards. Registering one grants a permanent bonus — a reduced version of what the card does when equipped.

RuleDetail
One registration per cardA second copy grants nothing on registration
Cards are not consumedRegistering deposits the card. It is still there.
Withdrawal is permittedTake it back any time
Bonus while withdrawnLost, until you put it back
Re-registeringFree

Withdrawal is what keeps the card market alive. Registering an expensive card is a decision rather than a sacrifice — you can pull it out to equip, to sell, or to fund something.

Consuming cards would take 1,023 items permanently out of an economy that produces them at 0.01%. Within a year the expensive ones would be gone and the market for them dead.

What completion is worth

Nobody will finish either one. Full completion needs roughly 10.2 million kills.

The server is balanced around the 25% column. Everything in the class and crafting design assumes that figure, not the theoretical maximum.

How it stacks

EffectStacking
Damage dealtAdditive
Healing doneAdditive
Flat statsAdditive
Damage reductionMultiplicative

Reduction walls at 100%, so each source applies to whatever is left. Added together, album plus gear plus research would reach effective immunity and nobody would ever die. Applied multiplicatively they reach something survivable.

Collection makes fights longer, not shorter. The album grants far more survivability than damage in proportion, so two equally progressed players take longer to kill each other than two fresh ones.

Dungeon bosses can be killed on demand; field bosses cannot. Without the reduction, a thirty minute dungeon run would be a better card farm than camping an MVP on an hour respawn.
