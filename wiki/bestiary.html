The bestiary

A permanent record of how many of each species you have killed. Reaching a milestone pays experience and a small permanent bonus, themed to that monster.

Kill enough of something and you get permanently better at the world. The bonuses are small individually and they never expire.

Milestones scale to what you are killing

ClassKill ladder
NormalThe full ladder, up to the highest milestone.
ChampionOne tenth of the normal ladder.
Mini-bossOne hundredth.
MVPOne hundredth.

Card drop rates sit around 0.01%, so ten thousand kills of an ordinary monster is a real goal a collector pursues rather than an absurd number. For MVPs and mini-bosses it would be absurd, which is why the ladder shortens.

What the rewards are

Small permanent stat bonuses, derived from what the monster's card does. A monster whose card gives attack pays a little attack. One whose card does something unscalable — movement speed, element change — pays something equivalent instead.

Rewards scale with how hard the monster is to farm. An MVP milestone is worth substantially more than a trash mob's, which is what makes hunting them for the bestiary worth doing on its own.

The card album

Every card can be registered once. Registering grants a permanent bonus — a reduced version of what the card itself does. Registering a second copy of the same card grants nothing.

That one-registration limit is what creates the market. There are over a thousand cards, all dropping at roughly 0.01%. Nobody finishes the album, ever. So there is permanent demand for cards nobody wants to equip, and the junk other servers vendor for pocket change has a buyer here.

The album is worth more than the bestiary

Deliberately. A card is a 0.01% drop; the bestiary is only persistence. Registering a card always pays more than the milestone for the same monster, so finding one still feels like finding one.

Both are character-wide

Progress is bound to the character, not the account. With multiclassing that is the same thing in practice — one character does everything, so the progress follows you into every class you hold.
