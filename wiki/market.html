Five auction houses, one aggregator, and a black market. Where every trade on the server happens.

Where you can sell

You can only list items at your own faction's auction house. A Geffen player sells at Geffen, always.

What you list is up to you. Materials you farmed in Morroc's dungeons go on Geffen's market if you are Geffen — nothing is restricted by where it came from.

Buying is open

Anybody can buy at any auction house by travelling there. Only selling is restricted.

Alberta's aggregator

Alberta's auction house shows every listing on the server, from all five factions, in one interface.

WhoConvenience fee
Alberta citizensNone
Everybody elseA percentage on top of the price and the seller's market tax

Buying direct is always cheaper. The aggregator costs money to avoid travelling, which means a player with time uses their own market and a player with zeny uses Alberta's.

The black market

Morroc has no separate auction house. Their black market is their auction house, with a second tab nobody else has.

TabWhat is on it
StandardOrdinary listings from Morroc citizens, like any market
Black marketGoods taken off intercepted caravans — gear and equipment rather than raw cargo
  • Anybody can buy there. You do not need to be Morroc.
  • Other factions can fence through Morroc for a cut, if they intercepted something.
  • Nowhere else sells it. Morroc is the only outlet on the server for stolen cargo.
  • Tier 5 gear appears here and nowhere else, and only when it came off a Metropolis faction's convoy.

Listing rules

Standard listingsTier 5 on the black market
Duration24 hours24 hours
FormatStarting bid plus optional buyoutBid only
MinimumThe item's base priceThe item's base price
Maximum200% of base priceNone

The floor stops somebody with ten thousand materials undercutting everybody to nothing. The ceiling stops the first person to corner a scarce card listing it at fifty times what it is worth.

Tier 5 has no ceiling because there is nothing comparable to price it against. It is the only genuinely scarce thing on the server, and bid-only means it goes to whoever wants it most rather than whoever refreshed fastest.

What can and cannot be listed

ItemListable?
Crafting materials, tiers 1 to 4Yes
CardsYes, freely
Ordinary equipment and dropsYes, as always
ConsumablesYes
Crafted gear, any tierNo. Soulbound when made.
Complete item drops from dungeonsNo. Soulbound on pickup.
Tier 5 materialsNo. Soulbound.
Tier 5 gearBlack market only, and only from an intercepted convoy
Only crafted gear is soulbound. Cards, ordinary drops, headgear and consumables all trade normally. The restriction exists so nobody can buy their way past the crafting system, not to shut down trade.

Taxes

Every sale pays a tax, and that tax becomes civic contribution for the city where the sale happened.

SaleTax
Standard listing15%
Black market30%
Tier 5 on the black market40%
Fencing through Morroc20% of the sale value, on top

Which means a tier 5 piece fenced by another faction and then sold loses 60% before anybody sees a coin. Morroc takes most of the value of goods they never touched.
