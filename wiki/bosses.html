The war

Caravans Sieges

Six server-wide PvE events. Everybody turns up and kills them together.

Nobody owns a world boss. PvP is disabled on these maps entirely. There is no faction restriction, no entry fee, no guild requirement, and nothing to contest — including between factions actively at war.

Unlocked by is not owned by. A faction reaching City tier is what puts their boss on the schedule. It gives them no claim to it, no priority, and no say in who attends.

Unlocking one

A boss appears once the faction that unlocks it reaches City tier, and stops spawning if that faction falls below it.

An accessory needs materials from all five bosses. If one faction is below City tier, that boss is not spawning, and nobody on the server can finish an accessory.

Which is worth knowing before you help push a faction down. Other services work the same way — Payon below City means no high-tier food or potions for anybody, Geffen below City means no transmutation, Morroc below City closes the black market.

Dungeon access is the exception. It never revokes, whatever happens to anybody's city tier.

Schedule

Detail
FrequencyOnce a week per faction boss
DayFixed and announced. Not Saturday — world bosses do not compete with sieges.
WindowServer-wide spawn announcement, then it is up until it dies
AttendanceUncapped. Four hundred people can be there if four hundred people turn up.

This is the one piece of content built for more than twenty players, and the health pools assume a crowd. It is a spectacle rather than an execution test — a dungeon boss punishes one person for standing wrong, a world boss has to still be a fight when a hundred people do.

Loot

Distributed by contribution, to everybody who contributed. Not to whoever landed the killing blow.

  • Anybody who genuinely fought receives something. More participation means more materials.
  • All of it is soulbound. World boss materials cannot be traded, sold or given away.
  • Nothing to tag and nothing to snipe. There is no advantage to being first, only to fighting.
MaterialFromUsed for
Accessory materialAll five faction bossesAccessories. One from each faction per item.
Epic materialAll five faction bossesThe epic crafting tier
Apex materialThe OneReserved — not a gear tier

Because the materials are soulbound there is no market for them. The only route to an accessory is turning up to all six fights, which means standing next to people you fought on Saturday.

The One

Level 290 with 275 million health — roughly seventy times the hardest thing in Glast Heim. It is the only monster in the game above the level cap.

ConditionDetail
Spawns whenAll five faction world bosses are defeated in the same week
WhereA neutral map nobody can deny access to
WhenThe following week, announced server-wide

Which needs all five bosses dead inside seven days. Nobody has to agree to anything — but The One does not appear unless every one of them went down, and a faction that sits out denies the whole server including themselves.

Mechanics at scale

The same vocabulary as dungeon bosses, tuned for a crowd.

MechanicHow it changes
AvoidMuch larger areas, longer telegraphs. Four hundred people cannot dodge a nine cell circle.
Converge and ScatterRadius scaled up substantially. Same instruction, different geometry.
Shield breakA damage gate rather than a timed check — a crowd breaks any shield instantly.
AddsContinuous and numerous. No add control at this scale, only area damage.
Armour crushRemoved. There is no designated tank in a fight this size.
LethalPresent, and it will kill a lot of people. Expect constant resurrection.

Which is also why the emote telegraphs matter more here, not less. Somebody at the back of a crowd of three hundred can see an emote. They cannot see a ground marker under two hundred sprites.
