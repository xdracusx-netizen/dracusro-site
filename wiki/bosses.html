World bosses

The one time nobody is at war. Strictly PvE, one arena per faction, and the deliberate release valve for everything else on this server.

The war

Caravans Sieges

Strictly PvE

No faction contest, no PvP flag, no fighting each other at the spawn. Anybody from any faction can walk in.

Everything else here is built to generate friction between factions. This is the common-threat moment where that gets set aside, and it exists on purpose.

Ordinary MVPs continue to exist as normal content. World bosses are a separate, rarer, server-scale tier that activates when your city reaches City.

The fight

Spawns are announced in advance on a scheduled window. Never random — random spawns produce overnight camping, which nobody enjoys.

The boss erupts from a fissure at the centre of the arena. The fissure is impassable terrain and keeps producing adds for the entire fight. The boss itself roams the open ground.

Resurrection is free. This is a fight about endurance, not about who can afford to keep standing.

Contribution is not just damage

If it were, healers and tanks would earn nothing in the one fight where they matter most.

ChannelWhat counts
DamageWhat you dealt to the boss.
HealingWhat you restored to other players.
MitigationDamage taken while holding the boss's attention.
SupportBuff and debuff uptime you kept running.

All four are normalised and summed. Everyone who shows up gets a baseline reward; the channels decide what you get above it.

Bringing more people is always correct

Contribution pools across your party. Twenty people pooling beats five people pooling. Bringing more players grows the reward rather than splitting it, so there is never a reason to keep the fight small or exclude anyone.

Which means no gatekeeping, no minimum gear requirements, and no reason to turn anyone away. A server-scale effort is the rewarded behaviour.

What drops

Epic-tier materials, which are supply-limited by schedule and cannot be farmed. These are the materials that upgrade endgame gear, which is why the real gear ceiling sits here rather than in the dungeons.

Civic and war contribution are awarded as well, so a world boss night develops your city and funds your guild at the same time.
