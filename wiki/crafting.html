The war

Caravans Sieges

Nothing is rolled. Modifiers are selected, materials always drop, and every item can be reforged into something else.

The rules everything follows from

  • Nothing is random. You choose your modifiers. No rerolling, no salvaging a bad outcome.
  • Materials always drop. 100% from the dungeon that produces them. Farming costs time, never luck.
  • Everything is reforgeable. A katar drop becomes a staff for whoever needs one.
  • Refining is unchanged. The existing in-game system stands as it is.
  • No faction makes anything exclusive. Factions differ in what it costs them, never in what exists.

Five pieces either way. Four armour plus a two-hander is five; four armour plus a one-hander and shield is also five. A class that cannot use shields is not locked out of its set bonus.

The gap between four and five is the largest. Four pieces is all your armour and no weapon, which a lot of players will settle for. The fifth is what separates finishing a tier from getting most of the way.

What you can put in them

CategoryTypes
DamagePhysical, magic, skill damage, healing done
DefencePhysical reduction, magic reduction, skill reduction
ElementsFire, water, wind, earth, holy, shadow damage
StatsSTR, AGI, VIT, INT, DEX, LUK — same number, so +15 instead of +15%

Nineteen types across five slots. You are choosing what to leave out, not just how to rank them.

Damage reduction can only sit on the lower rungs. The top two are offensive or healing only. Without that rule, six tier 5 slots of maximum reduction reaches effective immunity once the album and research are added, and sieges stop resolving.

Ten types across two accessories means everybody ends up with all of them. What differs is which sits at 15% and which at 4%, and two identically geared characters can play very differently.

Card slots

Every crafted item carries the maximum slots for its type. No hunting for a four-slot drop.

Four weapon slots is the largest driver of card demand in the game. Every character wants four in their weapon and five across their armour, and every card they want is also a card the album wants. Doing both properly means multiple copies of the same card against a 0.01% drop rate.

Materials

You need every faction's dungeon. Crafting a tier N piece requires materials from all five factions' tier N dungeons.

TierPer factionTotal per piece

Which means nobody gears up inside their own borders. Either you run four other factions' dungeons and pay the access fee, or you buy the materials from somebody who did. Every crafted item on the server represents materials from five factions changing hands.

Accessories need every world boss

Same rule. World bosses are open to everybody, so this is an invitation rather than a lockout — the materials are the reason to travel.

Complete items

Dungeons occasionally drop a finished piece instead of materials. It is random in slot and type, and because everything is reforgeable, it is never useless — it becomes whatever you need, or it gets sold to somebody who needs it.

What can be traded

Tradeable?
Crafting materials, tiers 1 to 4Yes
Crafted gearNo. Soulbound when made.
Complete item dropsNo. Soulbound on pickup.
Everything tier 5No. Gear and materials both.

A tradeable gear market would turn crafting into shopping. Materials trading gives the economy everything it needs without letting anybody buy a finished set.

Tier 5

Tier 5
Materials per piece25 — five from each Glast Heim wing
Drop rate100%, like every tier
TradingNone. Entirely soulbound.
SalvageDestroy a piece for 50% of its materials back, rounded up

Salvage is how a raid turns a piece nobody needed into progress toward one somebody does. Without it, a drop for a slot you already have is wasted permanently.

The one exception

Tier 5 taken off an intercepted Metropolis caravan is not soulbound — it was freight, not anybody's equipment. It can only be sold at Morroc's black market, by anybody, and that is the only route to tier 5 gear that does not involve clearing Glast Heim.

Faction advantages

FactionWhat they bring
PayonA 44% material discount at their forge, available to anybody who travels there and pays the tax. Alberta pays no tax.
GeffenTransmutation. Converts one faction's materials into another's at the same tier, down to 2:1 at full research.
MorrocThe only outlet for stolen tier 5.
AlbertaNo inter-faction tax anywhere, on anything.

Payon makes nothing exclusive. Exclusive recipes were considered and rejected — they would make Payon citizenship mandatory for crafters, and strand every faction relying on them if Payon fell behind.

Transmutation matters most when factions are out of step. If you have reached tier 4 and your neighbour is still on tier 3, their tier 4 materials do not exist yet. Geffen is the only route.
