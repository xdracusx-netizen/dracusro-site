The war

Caravans Sieges

Your city is the sum of what its citizens actually played. It is not bought, it is not gifted, and it cannot be rushed by a handful of people.

The five tiers

TierCumulative civicWhat it opens
Settlement—Tier 1 dungeon. Basic crafting. Food levels 1 and 2.
Village6,000,000Tier 2 dungeon. Better recipes. Food levels 3 to 5.
Town15,000,000Tier 3 dungeon. Food levels 6 to 8. Permanent floor.
City65,000,000Tier 4 dungeon. Research unlocks. Food levels 9 and 10, and Almighty.
Metropolis400,000,000Tier 5 access, the +20 food line, and the ability to carry tier 5 cargo.
Town is a floor you cannot fall below. Whatever happens in a war, your faction keeps its tier 3 dungeon and everything under it.

How long it takes

Modelled against twenty active players per faction. More people is faster; fewer is slower.

TierCasual paceCommitted pace
VillageAbout 2 weeksAbout 5 days
Town2 weeks after thatAbout a week
City7 weeks after that3 weeks
Metropolis15 weeks after that6 weeks

The curve accounts for players levelling as the city does. Early on a faction is killing level 60 monsters; by City tier they are on level 130 ones, which pay considerably more.

Upkeep

City and Metropolis require maintenance. Nothing below Town does.

TierDaily requirementActivity that needs
Town and belowNone—
City400,000 civic per dayAbout 28% of normal activity
Metropolis2,000,000 civic per dayAbout 41%

Fall short for seven consecutive days and the tier drops. An active faction never notices this. A faction that has stopped playing loses Metropolis in a week rather than a season.

Metropolis

The only tier that cannot be reached by contribution alone. You also have to take all five castles of another faction.

  • Tier 5 content opens — Glast Heim, and the best gear in the game.
  • Free access for the conquering faction, and for any other faction that also reaches Metropolis.
  • Everybody else pays a substantial entry fee, which goes to the Metropolis factions.
  • Your caravans can carry tier 5 cargo, which makes you a target worth robbing.
  • Lose the conquest and free access ends. The content stays open; it stops being yours.

Dungeon access

Your own faction's dungeons are free at or below your city tier. Everything else costs.

SituationCost
Your faction, at or below your tierFree
Your faction, above your current tierA fee per player, in zeny and civic
Another faction's dungeonA fee per player, paid to that faction

Access never revokes. Once a dungeon has opened for your city it stays open. Falling below the tier that unlocked it means paying to enter, not being locked out — because a hundred casual players should not lose the dungeon they run every evening because ten organised ones lost a fight on Saturday.

Tier 4 and 5 are the only ones that can be lost

Town is a permanent floor, so a faction can never be below tier 3. Below-tier fees only exist for the two tiers that can actually go away.

Holding somebody else's cities

Conquest transfers their military and faction research to you, up to whatever they had researched. Their economic research does not transfer.

Castles heldIncome efficiency
On capture50%
After one week75%
After two weeks100%

Which means territory taken and immediately contested is worth very little to anybody. A guild that takes a castle and loses it three days later gained almost nothing.
