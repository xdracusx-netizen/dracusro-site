Caravans Sieges

What every boss in the game does, and how to read it. There is no addon required and there never will be.

Reading a boss

No boss mod. No timers, no overlays, no third-party warnings. Every boss plays an emote one to two seconds before it acts, and the emote is consistent across all twenty-six encounters.
MechanicEmoteGround colour
Get outAnger or aggressionRed
Stack togetherBeckoning or summonBlue
Move onto the bossBeckoningPurple
Shield breakConcentration or charge-up—
Tank swapFocused, aimed at the current tank—
LethalDistinct from all of the above—
Puzzle resetLaugh or tauntGreen

Red means run away. Blue means run toward. Two colours, two opposite instructions, the same everywhere. A fight that requires an addon to read is a fight the designer failed to communicate.

The vocabulary

Every encounter is assembled from the same set. Learn them once.

Shield break instead of interrupts

Conventional interrupts are a skill check most classes fail by not having the skill. Instead, the boss raises a shield when it casts something dangerous — break the shield and the cast stops.

Which works for every class, because it is a damage check rather than a skill check. Five wizards can do it and five knights can do it.

Adds are not a mechanic

They are a property of the encounter, like the boss having hit points. Nothing is solved by killing them — they keep arriving and you deal with it while doing everything else.

TierAdds
1 to 3None or a trickle
4One wave, no respawn
5Respawning every 30 to 45 seconds

Tanking

Provoke is a hard taunt. The most recent application holds the boss permanently. Threat from damage does not override it.

Threat management was never interesting — a tank fighting their own damage dealers for aggro is a tax where the answer is always "do less damage". Tanks position, time cooldowns, and swap instead.

Armour crush

Tier 5 only. A stacking debuff on whoever holds the boss.

Effect
First applicationA large increase to damage taken. Survivable.
Second applicationDeath.
The swapThe other tank Provokes. The boss switches instantly and the first tank's stack decays.

Which needs two tanks, and the Prison wing needs two committed ones — Zealotus and Incubus both apply it, to separate tanks.

Phases and escalation

Every boss from tier 2 changes at 70% and 30% health. The mechanics do not change — their intensity does. More marked zones, faster casts, bigger shields, more people needed in a stack.

A group that learned the fight at full health is doing the same things at 30%, with less room and less time. Nothing new appears that you have not already seen.

Rules that apply everywhere

RuleDetail
Cleaving attacksEvery third auto-attack is a frontal cleave. Every second at tier 5.
Nothing resolves at onceIf two mechanics are due together, the second is delayed. Minimum two seconds between them.
Get-out mechanics are channelledAnd uninterruptible. The tank leaves too, which means the boss is briefly loose.
Avoidable damage hurtsIt was marked, telegraphed, and you had seconds. Standing in it should cost you.

Standing in front of a boss is always wrong unless you are the tank, and the tank faces it away from everybody. That is a rule you learn once and apply for the rest of the game.

Puzzles

From tier 4. Somebody leaves the fight to solve something while everybody else carries the damage.

TypeHow it works
RotationObjects rotate a fixed amount per click. Align them on a target.
TimingInteract with several objects within a short window of each other.
ChannelStand and channel, doing nothing else.
DestroyBring something down inside a window.
SequenceInteract in a specific, discoverable order.

They reset to a random state every time. The boss emotes, plays an area attack, and the room randomises. So the puzzle is solved rather than memorised — a group that ran the fight a hundred times still has to read the room.
