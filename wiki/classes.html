The war

Caravans Sieges

What has been changed from standard Ragnarok, and why. If you have played this game before, read this page.

The approach

Nothing here is a nerf for its own sake. Every change targets a specific pattern that makes fights worse for everybody involved.

PatternTreatment
No-cooldown mobilityCooldowns added, range increased in exchange
Uncapped burstCapped as a percentage of the target's maximum health
Damage that scales off the targetRemoved. Being tanky should not make you easier to kill.
Permanent denialDuration and cooldown added
Maintenance spamLong-duration party-wide buffs
Dead timeAfter-cast delay cut hard, replaced by visible cooldowns

Buffs last thirty minutes

Maintenance buffs are party-wide and last half an hour. Tactical ones stay short.

Long — 30 minutes, party-wideStill tactical
Blessing, Increase AGI, Angelus, Gloria, Magnificat, ImpositioKyrie Eleison, Assumptio, Suffragium
AspersioSafety Wall, Pneuma
Weapon Perfection, Adrenaline Rush, Power Thrust, Over ThrustMaximize Power
Elemental endows, Chemical ProtectionDefending Aura
Auto Guard, Reflect Shield—
  • Buffs persist through death. Rebuffing after every death in a siege is exactly the busywork being removed.
  • Lost on class change.
  • Dispel is now a real weapon. Stripping a thirty minute buff mid-fight is a serious blow.
  • Consumable buffs also persist through death. They are expensive.

Asura Strike

Capped as a share of the target's maximum health, and the cap depends on what you hit.

TargetCap
Players35% of maximum HP
Ordinary monsters70%, with no damage to anything below 30% health
Bosses5%, with no floor

Shattered Ki

Asura applies a mark to the target for 20 seconds. Everybody hitting that target deals 25% more damage, including the monk.

  • The target cannot be hit by another Asura while the mark holds.
  • SP cost is much lower than standard — a monk should manage around ten casts in a long fight.
  • In group content the mark is the point. Against a boss the monk's job is keeping it applied, which is worth far more than their personal damage.

Mobility

SkillChange
Body Relocation12 second cooldown. Range increased to 22 cells.
Backslide12 second cooldown. Range 10 to 12 cells, and you are concealed on use.

Backslide's concealment works at your own Cloaking level. A Rogue who never learned Cloaking still gets the reposition, just not the disappearing act.

Alchemist

Acid Demonstration no longer scales off the target's stats, is capped at 5% of maximum health, and has a 15 second cooldown.

It now applies Corroded — 20 seconds during which the target takes substantially more damage from every Alchemist ability, and Hell Plants hit considerably harder.

Which gives the class a rotation for the first time. Acid Demonstration sets up, everything else cashes in, and an alchemist pressing one button is now visibly playing badly.

Bard and Dancer

Songs and dances are now screen-wide auras centred on the performer, moving with them.

  • No positioning requirement. If you can see them, you have it.
  • One performer is enough. A second of the same song adds nothing.
  • Still channelled. They are not attacking or healing while it runs.

The old version was built for a game where fights happened in one spot. Nothing on this server stands still.

Three priests means three resurrections, staggered. The number of priests a raid brings is a real decision.

Crowd control

Repeated hard crowd control on the same target loses effect.

ApplicationDuration
First100%
Second50%
Third25%
FourthImmune
  • Resets after 20 seconds without further application.
  • Freeze, stun, sleep and stone curse share one counter. Rotating between them does not reset it.
  • It is per target, server-wide. Five players chaining on one person hit the wall as fast as one player would.

Animation and delay

Cast time is counterplay and it stays. After-cast delay is not, and it has been cut hard.

  • After-cast delay targets roughly half a second for standard skills.
  • Cooldowns replace long delays where a skill genuinely needs limiting. A cooldown is visible and plannable.
  • Movement cancels cleanly. You lose the skill if it has not fired, but you are not stuck.
  • Nothing locks you for longer than its cast time.
