The war

Caravans Sieges

What has been changed from standard Ragnarok, and why. If you have played this game before, read this page.

Third job is the ceiling. Fourth job is not on this server. Level cap is 200 base, 70 job.

The approach

Nothing here is a nerf for its own sake. Every change targets a specific pattern that makes fights worse for everybody involved.

PatternTreatment
No-cooldown mobilityCooldowns added, range increased in exchange
Uncapped burstCapped as a percentage of the target's maximum health
Damage that scales off your own HPRemoved everywhere. A tank should not also nuke.
Permanent denialDuration and cooldown added. A cooldown must always outlast the effect it creates.
Maintenance spamLong-duration party-wide buffs
Dead timeAfter-cast delay cut to about half a second, replaced by visible cooldowns

Rules differ by class on purpose. Each one is balanced on its own terms. They are not meant to play the same.

How cooldowns work here

  • Big area damage gets a long cooldown and hits proportionally harder. Landing one is a decision.
  • Single-target skills with real cast time keep their stock cooldowns. The cast is the limiter.
  • Every build has a low-cooldown filler, so long cooldowns never leave you standing there. Bread-and-butter skills sit at one to two seconds.
  • Cooldown reduction is a real build stat, and most of its value lands on the long ones.
  • Instant cast is achievable but hard. Fixed-cast reductions do not stack — the highest single source wins.

Kill refunds

Landing a killing blow returns 30% of that skill's cooldown. Players, monsters, adds, MVPs, summons, structures — anything killable, in PvP and PvE alike.

Applies toNever applies to
Finishers, damage skills, area damageCrowd control
Pops — EDP, Unlimit, Inspiration, Telekinesis IntenseHeals and defensive skills
—Buffs, mobility, Resurrection, Dispel

Which punishes clumping hard. A caster who kills four people with one area skill has it back immediately. Groups have to answer strikers by spreading out, not by stacking.

  • No cap and no floor. Chain kills, chain refunds.
  • Dying costs nothing extra. Your timers keep running.
  • Supports rarely benefit. Arch Bishops, Royal Guards and performers seldom land killing blows, which is consistent with their job.
  • Summons and structures are refund fodder for the enemy. Spreading them out is not optional.

Buffs

Four tiers, and which one a buff sits in decides everything about it.

TierDurationOn deathDispelExamples
Maintenance30 minutes, party-widePersistsRemovedBlessing, Increase AGI, Angelus, Impositio, Gloria, Magnificat, Aspersio, endows, Adrenaline Rush, Weapon Perfection, Over Thrust, Auto Guard, Reflect Shield, Chemical Protection
TacticalShortRemovedRemovedKyrie, Assumptio, Suffragium, Praefatio, Offertorium, King's Grace
PopShort window, long cooldownRemovedImmuneEDP, Unlimit, Inspiration, Telekinesis Intense
PerformanceUp to 30 minutesEndsDispelling the performer ends itMinstrel and Wanderer songs and dances
  • Maintenance buffs persist through death. Rebuffing after every death in a siege is exactly the busywork being removed.
  • All buffs are lost on class change.
  • Pops cannot be dispelled. They are short and expensive; stripping them would make them pointless.
  • Performance auras end when the performer dies, or when somebody dispels them.
  • Self-buffs that gate a class cast fast, so rebuilding them never breaks combat.

Dispel and Clearance reach 5 cells. Stripping buffs means walking into the enemy team, which is the cost of doing it.

Which means stacking a class is fine if you spread it and wasteful if you concentrate it. Two Suras belong on two objectives, not on one emperium.

Asura Strike

Rebuilt on ATK. No sphere cost, no HP or SP scaling, 20 second cooldown. Fury still required.

ContextCap
Open world — monsters, minibosses and MVPs70% of maximum HP, with no damage below 30% target health
PvP35% of maximum HP
Scripted encounters — dungeon and world bosses5%, with no floor

Shattered Ki

Asura marks the target for 20 seconds.

EffectValue
Damage from all sources+25%
Tiger Cannon and Gate of Hell against the marked targetDouble damage
Further AsuraThe target is immune while the mark holds, from anybody
RemovalCannot be dispelled or cleansed. Nothing removes it.

Which is the Sura's actual job. Asura is not the kill — it is the setup. The kill is Gate of Hell or Tiger Cannon landing inside the window, and everybody else hitting that target harder while it holds.

Open world MVPs keep the 70% cap, so a Sura stays a genuine menace to anything roaming a field map. Everybody can be a Sura, so that is not an exclusive advantage.

Damage never scales off your own health

A tank that also nukes is removed everywhere. Dragon Breath, Death Bound, Martyr's Reckoning, Rage Burst, Self-Destruction, Gate of Hell and Asura are all rebuilt on attack power instead.

Scaling off the target's health is fine, and deliberately kept — it is the anti-tank tool that keeps pace with card album health. Poison Smoke, Crescent Elbow, the Gate of Hell execute and Acid Demonstration's cap all work this way.

Crowd control

Repeated crowd control on the same target loses effect, and almost everything shares one counter.

ApplicationDuration
First100%
Second50%
Third25%
FourthImmune

Resets after about 20 seconds without a new application. Per target, server-wide — five players chaining hit the wall as fast as one.

In the shared counterSeparate counterNot crowd control
Stun, freeze, sleep, stone, Crystallize, Fear, every root, every hold, every silence, charm and confusionStrip and Disable — 5s, then 2.5s, then 1.25s, then immune. Kept separate so Rogues keep their edge.Slow, blind, curse, Burning, Freezing, hit and flee debuffs, the soft poisons, performance debuffs
  • Nothing locks a player for more than six seconds. Crowd control creates an opening; it is never an I-win button.
  • Fear is a 2 second root plus reduced hit and flee. It does not block attacks or skills.
  • Divest All counts as one application, not several.

Players never break equipment

Every player-caused break — Meltdown, Acid Terror, Acid Demonstration, Weapon Crush, card procs — becomes Disable instead. The item stays equipped, its stats and requirements switch off for 5 seconds, then it works again. It counts toward the strip counter.

Self-break chances on Crush Strike and Maximum Over Thrust are removed entirely. Monsters keep real breaks, so repair skills stay useful — but no dungeon or world boss encounter is given one.

Neither wall blocks magic. The answers to magic area damage are Land Protector, silences, Spell Breaker, dispelling the caster's buffs, and spreading out.

Everything that makes defending easy gets a short window. Otherwise sieges always favour the defender.

Summons, structures and traps

Summons are an extension of the player, not a replacement.

BehaviourCap
Mobile summonsAttack only what their owner is attacking. Leashed.2 Flora, 2 Marine Sphere, 1 spirit
StructuresFire at the Mechanic's target when in range2 Silver Sniper, 2 Magic Decoy
HomunculusAutonomous, as always1
Ranger traps—3 at a time. Placing a fourth destroys the oldest.
  • All summons combined sit at 50–60% of what the caster does alone. A full set supplements you; it does not replace you.
  • Mechanic structures reach 80–100%, because they are stationary and the Mechanic is built around them.
  • Summon stats scale off caster attack power, never off health.
  • An idle player's summons do nothing. No target, no action.

Rules that catch people out

  • No gear swapping in instanced PvP. Your loadout locks when you enter a siege or a caravan. Ammunition swaps freely, and anything stripped off you can be re-equipped.
  • Autoloot is off by default, and it never picks up cards or anything under a 1% drop rate.
  • MVP credit is by damage contribution. No first-hit tag, no last-hit snipe. Everybody who contributed gets something and the top contributors get more.
  • Consumables are a cost, not a limiter. The cooldown is the limiter — buying a thousand bottles gives the same uptime as buying thirty.
