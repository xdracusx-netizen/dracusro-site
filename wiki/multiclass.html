Mastery bonuses

Reaching maximum job level in a class permanently increases one stat, for that character, in every class they play.

Job tier reachedBonus
First job+2
Second job+4
Third job+6
Fourth job+8
  • Awarded at maximum job level, not when you unlock the class.
  • The highest rank replaces the lower within a line. Mastering fourth job gives +8, not +20.
  • Sixteen per stat at full mastery, ninety-six across all six.
  • It applies in PvP. A character who mastered everything is measurably stronger than one who did not.

Switching class

Free, at the multiclass interface. There is no cost and no cooldown.

Where you cannot switchWhy
Mid-dungeonYou committed to a composition
Mid-siegeSame
On a caravan routeSame

Buffs are lost on class change. The character that was buffed no longer exists in that form.

Gear and reforging

Your gear changes with you. Reforging converts an item into a different template within the same tier, keeping its refine level and modifiers.

RuleDetail
What changesThe item's type and its base statistics
What survivesRefine level, tier, modifiers, and set membership
Tier is fixedA tier 3 item becomes a different tier 3 item, never a tier 4
CostOne crafting material per slot, of that item's tier
WhereAnywhere you can change class

Set bonuses survive reforging, which is what makes multiclassing affordable. A tier 4 set stays a set whether it is currently a two-hander or a one-hander and shield.

Loadouts

Thirty-two saved configurations per character, named by you. Applying one reforges every slot in a single action, for the same materials it would cost to do manually.

Monks and Assassins never carry a shield. Both are built around committing to offence with no defensive fallback, and a monk with a shield is a monk who has stopped having a weakness.

A two-handing Crusader gives up their shield skills. Shield Boomerang, Shield Charge, Auto Guard and Reflect Shield all need one. So the spear build trades most of the class's defensive kit for reach and Spear Quicken — which is a real decision rather than a strictly better option.

Without these restrictions reforging would be the exploit — bolt a shield onto anything, swap into whatever is strongest this week, and class identity lasts about a fortnight.

Trainee gear

Given twice per character, ever. Once at first job, once at second. It has no value, cannot be sold, and cannot be traded.
