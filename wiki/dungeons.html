Five tiers

Four dungeons per faction plus a shared endgame, each unlocked by your city reaching the matching development tier.

TierOpens atNotes
Tier 1SettlementTwo encounter chambers. Legible on a first run without a map.
Tier 2VillageThree chambers, more trash between them.
Tier 3TownFour chambers.
Tier 4CityFive chambers, the largest per-faction dungeon.
Tier 5Metropolis + conquestShared by all factions. The endgame.

Access follows the access rule: once a dungeon opens for your city it never closes, and using one above your tier or belonging to another faction costs a fee rather than being blocked.

Solo or party

Every dungeon can be cleared alone. The party version pays better drops and an experience bonus — and the experience matters more, because experience is what feeds your city.

The party version also has mechanics the solo version does not. Fights that need two people in two places, damage that outpaces what you can heal through yourself. Not simply bigger numbers.

Why not just a higher drop rate. A better drop rate has never kept anyone grouped — they solo it twice instead. Grouping has to be the only way to do something, not the faster way to do everything.

Where the ceiling actually is

Base gear drops freely and always will. Farming it is unlimited, and that is not where the constraint lives.

The materials that upgrade the good equipment come from world bosses and from holding territory. Both happen on a schedule and neither can be ground out. What your faction won last week decides what you can wear this week.

Refining

Protection ore only. No blessed stones, no enriched ore, no shortcuts. You never destroy the item — you spend the ore.

Which puts the gear ceiling on supply rather than on luck or on your wallet.

Crafting

Each city tier opens recipes that were not available before, and Payon reduces the material cost on all of it. The dungeon that opened at Town drops the materials that the crafting Town unlocked turns into gear — the two are designed to arrive together.
