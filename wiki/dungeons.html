Every chamber before the last holds a miniboss. The last holds the dungeon's MVP with scripted mechanics. Tiers 1 to 3 are genuinely soloable; tier 4 assumes a full party and tier 5 a full raid.

Finding one

There is no queue. Every dungeon sits where it has always sat, with an NPC at the entrance who tells you a new chamber has been found, takes the fee, and opens the warp.

A queue makes a dungeon a button. A guard at the mouth of Anthell makes it a place you travelled to.

Losing forty minutes of progress to a mechanic nobody had seen teaches people to look up a guide or stop running the dungeon.

Solo and group

Every dungeon can be cleared alone at tiers 1 to 3. The party version is not the same content with more people — it has mechanics the solo version does not.

A higher drop rate has never kept anybody grouped; they solo it twice instead. Grouping has to be the only way to do something, so the party version needs two people in two places.

What a run gives

  • Base and job experience.
  • Civic and military contribution at the standard rate. Dungeon monsters are monsters.
  • Crafting materials, always, at 100%.
  • Bestiary progress like anything else.
  • Cards, at reduced rates. See the bestiary page.

Dungeons supply the common and rare material tiers. The genuinely scarce materials come from world bosses and territory, which is what puts the gear ceiling somewhere that cannot be farmed.
