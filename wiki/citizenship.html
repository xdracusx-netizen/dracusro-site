Citizenship

Not a chat channel and not a cosmetic tag. Your faction decides which city your work builds, which content is cheap for you, and who you are at war with on Saturday.

What it decides

Every faction has its own city to develop, its own dungeons, its own world boss, its own gear to farm and its own cosmetics. Picking one is the first real decision you make.

  • Where your contribution goes. Every kill feeds the city you belong to.
  • What you can reach cheaply. Your own faction's dungeons cost nothing extra. Everyone else's charge an access fee.
  • Who you fight. Sieges are between factions. You cannot fight for one you do not belong to.

The five factions

Each holds one thing the other four need, which is what makes diplomacy real rather than decorative. Cutting anybody off costs you something too.

FactionHoldsWhat that means in practice
PronteraGovernanceCollects double the tax it sets. Harder to invade than anyone else, and needs less development than normal before it can declare war.
MorrocSubterfugeHigher return on intercepted caravans, and the black market where stolen cargo finds a buyer. Rich in war supply, slow to build cities.
GeffenResearchReaches three tiers past everyone else on every research track, and converts the same investment into more progress.
PayonProductionReduced material cost on crafting, enhancing and refining, plus recipes nobody else can make. Everybody gears through Payon.
AlbertaTradePays no inter-faction tax at all, sells into every city, and can field any other faction's siege hardware.

Nobody should join the biggest one

City development is measured per active citizen, not in raw total.

A faction that swells far past its share develops more slowly per person. Which means stampeding into whoever looks strongest actively makes them weaker, and you are better off somewhere that needs you.

Why this exists. Without it, the largest faction wins permanently and everyone piles in. Measuring per head turns that into a self-correcting problem instead of a death spiral.

Leaving is possible, and deliberately slow

You cannot switch until the city you belong to has reached City tier. Nobody gets to abandon a settlement the moment it looks like hard work.

After that, every move costs more than the last.

MoveCooldown before the next one
First switch1 month
Second switch3 months
Third switch9 months

Whatever you contributed stays with the city you contributed it to. This exists so a faction losing badly can bleed players — not so you can chase whoever is winning this week.

Your Discord role is not your citizenship

The faction role on Discord is a colour and a channel. It changes freely and instantly. Your actual citizenship lives in game and follows the rules above.
