Choosing a faction

Every character belongs to one of five factions. The choice is made at character creation and it decides where you can craft, which auction house you can sell at, which research you benefit from, and who you are at war with.

FactionSeatSecondaryIdentity
PronteraPronteraAldebaranGovernance. Collects double the tax it sets, walls are harder, declares war at a lower threshold.
MorrocMorrocComodoSubterfuge. Higher return on intercepted caravans, runs the black market, climbs walls without ladders.
GeffenGeffenJunoResearch. Three extra ranks on every shared research node. Fields magic artillery.
PayonPayonAmatsuProduction. Cheaper crafting for everybody who uses their forge, and they take a cut.
AlbertaAlbertaLouyangFree trade. Pays no inter-faction tax anywhere, and reaches every auction house from one place.

National perks are active from the first day

You do not research your way into them. A Payon citizen crafts 20% cheaper from the moment they log in, and research stacks on top of that.

Leaving

You can defect to another faction. It costs progressively more each time.

DefectionCooldown before you can move again
First1 month
Second3 months
Third and after9 months
  • Your contribution stays behind. Everything you earned for your old city remains theirs.
  • You are removed from your guild automatically.
  • Your city tier gates it. You cannot defect out of a faction that is behind.
  • Your character is unchanged. Levels, gear, bestiary and album all come with you.

Contribution

Everything you kill pays two currencies to your faction alongside your own experience.

CurrencyWhere it comes fromWhat it pays for
CivicAlmost entirely from killing thingsCity development, research, dungeon access, caravan commissioning
MilitaryA small trickle from kills, and mostly from caravansDeclaring war, siege equipment, respawn tickets

Higher level monsters pay more, but not proportionally more. A level 180 monster is worth roughly nine times a level 100 one rather than seventy-eight times, which is deliberate — a faction full of endgame players should be stronger, not the only thing that counts.

You cannot grind your way to a war. Military points come overwhelmingly from caravans. A faction that never trades can build a Metropolis and still not afford to attack anybody.

Research

Unlocked when your city reaches City tier. Three trees, each with six nodes everybody can take and one exclusive to your faction.

TreeWhat it covers
MilitarySiege assault and defence, caravan raiding and escort, plunder, logistics
EconomicField yield, refining, civic works, secondary territory, trade contracts
FactionMonster slaying, resilience, boss assault, spoils, expedition

Ranks

Every node has five ranks. Each one costs more than the last, so taking one node to rank 5 costs about as much as taking four nodes to rank 2.

Geffen reaches rank 8 on shared nodes only. Exclusive nodes cap at rank 5 for everybody including them. Their extra depth costs roughly four and a half times what a normal node does, so they specialise brutally rather than being better at everything.

Where the funding comes from

SourceSplit
Citizens30% to research, 70% to the city, while research is active
Castle-holding guilds50% to research, 50% to the city

Guild leaders direct research continuously. Citizens nominate a node once a week in a 24 hour window, and the top contributor gets the nomination.

Guilds

RuleDetail
Maximum size20

CastlesFive per faction, one guild each

DisbandingThe castle reverts to unclaimed. Research ranks stay with the faction.

Approval and civil war

Every citizen votes on their castle-holding guilds weekly, not just guild members. Approval ratings are public.

ApprovalStatus
70% or aboveSecure
Below 70%Vulnerable to challenge
  • Only landless guilds may challenge. A guild that already holds a castle cannot take another by civil war.
  • The challenger is whoever contributed most in economic and military terms that week.
  • Civil wars are fought in the second hour of the siege window, alongside rebellions.
  • A successful defence buys a week of safety and leaves approval unchanged.

Declaring war

All five castle-holding guild leaders must vote yes. One refusal and there is no war.

TargetCost
Any standard faction260,000 military points
Prontera460,000 military points

A declaration commits every guild in the faction. All five attack all five of the target's castles in the same hour. There is no partial commitment and no guild held in reserve.

Which means a faction whose five guild leaders cannot agree is a faction that does not go to war. That is not an accident of the design — it is the design.
