Cosmetics

You see it, you know what it costs, you buy that thing.

No boxes. No spins. No odds. Nothing here changes a stat, a drop rate, or the outcome of a fight. Every item below is bought outright, in game, at a price you can read before you spend anything.

How it works

1

Donate

Through the control panel. You receive Proof of Donation in the mail, in game.

2

Collect

PoD arrives by RODEX. It is account-bound — it cannot be traded, sold, or turned into zeny.

3

Spend

Visit the cosmetics vendor in any major city and trade PoD for whatever you actually want.

2 PoD = $1 USD. Fixed, always. Nothing is ever on sale, discounted, bundled, or time-limited, because that is how you end up with people buying things they did not want.

The rules we are not going to break

  • Nothing sold here affects gameplay. No stats, no drop rates, no experience, no movement speed, no storage space. Cosmetic means cosmetic.
  • No randomised containers. Not boxes, not spins, not "chance to receive". If a thing is for sale, it is for sale by itself at a number you can read.
  • PoD is account-bound. It cannot be traded or sold, so donations never turn into zeny and money never becomes an advantage in the game.
  • Prices do not move. No flash sales, no limited-time pricing, no urgency engineering. The thing costs what it costs.
  • Cash shop experience and drop multipliers are disabled entirely. They exist in the emulator. They are switched off.
