What we took out

This server is easier to describe by what is missing. Everything below exists on most Ragnarok servers and none of it is here.

Quest chains

No Eden board, no attunement, no unlock chains, no dailies, no weekly reset. You decide where to go and what to do. Nothing here expires if you miss a day.

Inflated rates

Base and job experience run at 1×. Cards drop at 0.01%. Nothing you own arrived because a multiplier handed it to you.

Anything you can buy to win

No gacha, no lootboxes, no gambling of any kind. Cash shop experience and drop multipliers are disabled entirely. The shop sells cosmetics at a fixed price and nothing else.

Alt armies

One character holds every class, so there is nothing to gain from a second one. The character you build is the character you play — in a dungeon, in a siege, anywhere.

A world that never changes

Your city levels up as its citizens play, and each level unlocks things that were not there before — deeper dungeons, better crafting, its own world boss.

Filler classes

Human classes only, all the way through 4th job. No Doram, no Baby classes. Fewer things to balance means the ones that exist get looked at properly.

Pick a side once

Citizenship is a commitment, not a cosmetic. Each faction has its own city to build, its own dungeons and world boss, its own gear to farm and its own cosmetics — and holds one thing the other four need.

Prontera

Governance · Aldebaran

The trade hub, and the hardest city on the map to invade. Every road runs through it, which makes Prontera rich and permanently surrounded.

Collects double the tax it sets. Needs more development to be attacked than anyone else, and less to declare war.

Morroc

Subterfuge · Comodo

The richest war economy and the slowest cities. Morroc takes what it cannot build, and its black market is where stolen cargo finds a buyer.

Higher return on intercepted caravans. Infiltrates walls without ladders.

Geffen

Research · Juno

Turns war supply into permanent advantage faster than anyone. Where other factions cap a research track, Geffen keeps going.

Reaches three tiers past every other faction on siege, field and governance research.

Payon

The Forge · Amatsu, Louyang

Everybody gears through Payon. Diplomatic weight without needing to win a single fight — which is a strange kind of power to hold.

Reduced material cost on crafting, enhancing and refining. Recipes nobody else can produce.

Alberta

Free Trade · The islands

The market. Alberta sells into every city, pays nobody's tax, and can field any other faction's siege equipment.

Exempt from all inter-faction tax. Buys any faction's exclusive war hardware.

How the world moves

Four systems that feed each other. None of them work alone, and all of them are driven by what players actually did last week. The full guide explains each one properly — what it is, how it works, and what it asks of you.

CITIES

Settlements become metropolises

Every kill, every clear, every delivery pays into your city. Five tiers, each one a different map with more of everything. What your city unlocks — dungeons, crafting, the tax you can charge — depends on how much the population actually played.

WAR

Sieges across three stages

Twenty against twenty. Take the wall, then the courtyard, then the throne room. Ground you hold bleeds the defender, so nobody wins by standing still. The emperium takes area damage, so nobody wins by piling on top of it either.

CLASSES

One character, every class

Unlock a class once and it stays unlocked. Swap between them at your faction city, keeping the same character, the same name, and everything you have collected. There is no reason to build a second one.

COLLECTION

Every monster is worth killing

A bestiary that tracks kills per species and pays out permanently. An album where every card registers once, so the second Poring Card you find is worth something to somebody. There are 1,023 cards. Nobody is going to finish.

The shop

Costumes and cosmetics. You see the item, you see the price, you buy the item.

No boxes. No spins. No odds.

Nothing in this shop affects a stat, a drop rate, or a fight. Nothing is randomised, and nothing is sold in a container you open hoping for something better. If a thing is for sale, it is for sale by itself, at a number you can read before you pay.
